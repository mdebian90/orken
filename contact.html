ABOUT // ØRKEN

Ørken is the creative identity of Øliver Cruz, an Interactive Media Digital Design (DDMI) student at the Institute of Architecture, Design and Art (IADA) of the Autonomous University of Ciudad Juárez (UACJ). Throughout this academic and professional path, multidisciplinary training has bridged visual design and interactive software development across multiple fields. Experience encompasses 3D animation, modeling, texturing, and rendering using Blender and Autodesk Maya, videogame branding, level design and interactive game mechanics built in Unity Engine with C# and Adobe Photoshop, web and mobile UI/UX interface architecture developed with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Swift and Kotlin, as well as digital graphic compositions, vector illustration, and audiovisual editing with Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator and Adobe Premiere Pro.
